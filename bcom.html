About B.Com Computer Applications

The Bachelor of Commerce in Computer Applications (B.Com CA) programme integrates core commerce subjects with computer applications, enabling students to gain expertise in Accounting, Finance, Taxation, Business Management, Programming, Database Management, Web Technologies, and Information Systems. The programme prepares graduates for careers in both the business and IT sectors through practical learning and industry-focused education.
